01

Find thenight.

Open VIBE and you are looking at Budapest tonight. Every pin is a venue with something on. Tap one to see the event, who is playing and whether the doors are open.

  • Clusters open up as you zoom in
  • Distance to every venue from where you are
  • Set a radius to see only what is close
  • Pop-up locations appear when they are announced

02

Know therunning order.

Every event has its full lineup, billed the way the flyer bills it. When set times are out you get them room by room, with whoever is on right now marked live.

  • Headliners, support, B2Bs and live sets
  • Festival days as separate tabs
  • See how many people are going
  • Got the timetable as a screenshot? Upload it and VIBE reads the set times

Walk straight in.

03

Walk

straight in.

Buy from the official seller, then share the ticket PDF into VIBE. Your QR opens in one tap, works with no signal, and the screen brightens for the scanner.

04

Rate thenight.

The morning after, VIBE asks how it was: the night, the vibe and each set you caught. Only people who were there can answer, with a ticket in the wallet or a check-in at the venue.

Venues are scored on sound, crowd, lineup, door, bar wait and safety.
